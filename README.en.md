# ReachRich

ReachRich is a research platform for China A-shares. This repository provides product information, research-method resources and links to the user guide.

[ReachRich](https://reachrich.ai/) · [Platform](https://reachrich.ai/about) · [User Guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/) · [Help](https://reachrich.ai/support)

## Keep a useful research record

Record the security identifier, original source, data date, first-available time, price-adjustment convention and research question. Keep quoted facts separate from your interpretation. Reuse these definitions when comparing later observations.

ReachRich provides the platform and research resources. [RiQi](https://riqi.ai/) is the research assistant for organizing questions and following up on supplied materials. [Start with a company-research workflow](https://riqi.ai/research/a-share-company-research.html).

This repository contains product information and research guides, not the product backend, production datasets or proprietary strategies. The current commercial focus is China A-shares; Hong Kong and US equities remain under development.
