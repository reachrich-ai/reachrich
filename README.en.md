ReachRich is a quantitative research platform for China A-shares. It brings market data, news and stock screening into one place for individual researchers and institutional teams. [RiQi](https://github.com/reachrich-ai/riqi) is its AI research assistant for discussing companies and supporting documents.

[Open ReachRich](https://reachrich.ai/) · [Documentation](https://docs.reachrich.ai/) · [Platform overview](https://reachrich.ai/about) · [简体中文](README.md)

## Get started

1. **Review the market.** Check indices and market breadth, then look at a company's price and volume.
2. **Build a candidate list.** Start from news or a screening template. Keep the research question, conditions and data date with the result.
3. **Investigate further.** Take the relevant disclosures and a specific question to [RiQi](https://riqi.ai/). Discuss changes in the business and follow up on the evidence behind the answer.

See [your first A-share research task](https://docs.reachrich.ai/start/quickstart.html) and the [company research walkthrough](https://docs.reachrich.ai/workflows/company-study.html).

## Features

- **Market data**: review price and volume in [daily charts](https://docs.reachrich.ai/reachrich/daily-k.html) and [weekly or monthly views](https://docs.reachrich.ai/reachrich/weekly-monthly.html).
- **News research**: search by keyword, source and category, then check the original announcement or disclosure. [Guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/reachrich/news.html)
- **Stock screening**: use templates to build candidate lists, keeping conditions and data dates available for further research. [Guide](https://docs.reachrich.ai/reachrich/screener.html)
- **Conversational research**: discuss documents in RiQi, question the evidence, and return to the same conversation with new information. [About RiQi](https://reachrich.ai/riqi)

## Research guides

- [A-share backtesting: costs and price adjustments](https://reachrich.ai/research/a-share-backtest-costs.html): compare price conventions, execution assumptions, turnover and out-of-sample results.
- [From news to company announcements](https://reachrich.ai/research/a-share-news-event-study.html): align publication and event dates, gather original documents and identify what to watch next.
- [Factor validation and overfitting](https://reachrich.ai/research/a-share-factor-validation.html): record parameter trials, fix validation periods and check costs and execution constraints.
- [A-share company research with RiQi](https://riqi.ai/research/a-share-company-research.html): work from company disclosures to follow-up questions.

## Documentation and support

[User handbook](https://docs.reachrich.ai/) · [Account and feature access](https://docs.reachrich.ai/help/access.html) · [Support](https://reachrich.ai/support)

The current commercial focus is China A-shares. Features and data depend on account permissions; Hong Kong and US markets remain in development. Contact support for institutional trials or product questions. The linked handbook and research guides are currently in Chinese.
